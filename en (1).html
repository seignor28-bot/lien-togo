How to pay from abroad

You pay by money transfer to our Mobile Money number. The number is sent to you with the quote on WhatsApp.

  1. Receive your quote in euros on WhatsApp.
  2. Send the 50% deposit with a money transfer app (for example Western Union, WorldRemit, Remitly or Sendwave). Check that it sends to Togo from your country.
  3. Send us the proof of payment on WhatsApp.
  4. We carry out the mission and send you photos and a receipt. The balance is paid at the end.
